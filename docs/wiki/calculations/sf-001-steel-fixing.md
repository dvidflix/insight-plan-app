# SF-001 Steel fixing (12 men, 2 shifts)

## Inputs

| Input | Value | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Steel fixers | 12 | men |
| Shifts | 2 | shifts |
| Hours per week | 55 | h |
| Working hours per day | 9.17 | h |
| Productivity | 75 | Mh/t |

## Calculations

| # | Step | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gang hours per week | Fixers × Shifts × Hours per week = 12 × 2 × 55 | 1,320 Mh/week |
| 2 | Tonnage per week | Gang hours ÷ Productivity = 1,320 ÷ 75 | 17.6 t/week |
| 3 | Tonnage per day | Fixers × Shifts × Hours per day ÷ Productivity = 12 × 2 × 9.17 ÷ 75 | 2.93 t/day |

## Summary

| Weekly output | Daily output |
|---|---|
| **17.6 t/week** | **2.93 t/day** |
