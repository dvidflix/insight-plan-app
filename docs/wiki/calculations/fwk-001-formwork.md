# FWK-001 Formwork (14 men, 1 shift)

## Inputs

| Input | Value | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Formwork area | 500 | m² |
| Carpenters | 14 | men |
| Shifts | 1 | shifts |
| Hours per week | 55 | h |
| Productivity | 6 | Mh/m² |
| Working days per week | 6 | days |

## Calculations

| # | Step | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total man-hours | Area × Productivity = 500 × 6 | 3,000 Mh |
| 2 | Gang hours per week | Carpenters × Shifts × Hours per week = 14 × 1 × 55 | 770 Mh/week |
| 3 | Duration | Total man-hours ÷ Gang hours per week = 3,000 ÷ 770 | 3.90 weeks |
| 4 | Duration in days | Weeks × Days = 3.90 × 6 | 23.4 days |
| 5 | Output per week | Area ÷ Weeks = 500 ÷ 3.90 | 128 m²/week |

## Summary

| Area | Man-hours | Duration | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **500 m²** | **3,000 Mh** | **23.4 days** | **128 m²/week** |
