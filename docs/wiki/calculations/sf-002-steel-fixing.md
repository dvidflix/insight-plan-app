# SF-002 Steel fixing (7 men, 1 shift)

## Inputs

| Input | Value | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Steel fixers | 7 | men |
| Shifts | 1 | shifts |
| Hours per week | 55 | h |
| Working hours per day | 9.17 | h |
| Productivity | 75 | Mh/t |

## Calculations

| # | Step | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gang hours per week | Fixers × Shifts × Hours per week = 7 × 1 × 55 | 385 Mh/week |
| 2 | Tonnage per week | Gang hours ÷ Productivity = 385 ÷ 75 | 5.13 t/week |
| 3 | Tonnage per day | Fixers × Shifts × Hours per day ÷ Productivity = 7 × 1 × 9.17 ÷ 75 | 0.86 t/day |

## Summary

| Weekly output | Daily output |
|---|---|
| **5.13 t/week** | **0.86 t/day** |
