Pricing questions

Why per user, not per project?

The tool is used by individual planners, so the cost follows the people who use it. There is no fee to add a project, no limit on models or programmes, and nothing to renegotiate when the next project starts.

Which licence do I need?

Programmes if your work is in the schedule: checks, comparison, resources and look-ahead. Models if it is in the IFC model: checks, clashes, sets and review. Both if you want to link them, play the build in 4D, or check the programme against the model. You can upgrade at any time.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Start a free 30-day trial on your own programme and model, with set-up help if you want it. That tells you more than a demo. A trial key works like a licence key and runs out on its own.

Do you need a server or an IT project?

No. It is a web app: open it in Chrome or Edge, install it from the browser, and it works offline. A licence key is checked on your computer, so there is no sign-in and no licence server. If you prefer, your organisation can host the files on its own intranet.

Where is our data kept?

In the project folder you choose, on your computer. The app does not send programmes or models anywhere, and has no account or telemetry.

What happens when the licence ends?

The app shows a warning in the last 30 days, and you can renew with a new key. If you do not renew, the app stops opening, but your files are still yours: programmes and models are in your folder in open formats, and the project database is an ordinary SQLite file.

What if a colleague only needs to look at results?

Viewer-only access is planned. Tell us if it matters to you.

What support is included?

Updates for the licence term, the in-app guide, and email support. Priority support and onboarding are available for larger roll-outs.
