Common questions

Why price per project?

Construction budgets are set per project. A project licence matches how you buy, and avoids counting every person who looks at a result.

What happens after the pilot?

You keep your files, since they stay on your machine. If you continue, the pilot fee is credited against the first project licence.

Do you need a server or an IT project?

No. It installs from a single folder on a Windows laptop and works offline.

Is a free trial available?

The pilot is the trial: a short, fixed-fee engagement on your own data, which tells you more than a demo.

Run a pilot on a real project

Bring a live programme and its model. We will set up a short, fixed-price pilot and show what it finds.
