Set up your first project

About 5 minutes. The full text is in the documentation.

  1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge and paste your licence key, then press Activate. It is checked on your computer, so you only enter it once.
  2. Press Choose folder… and pick a folder on your computer or in OneDrive. The browser asks permission once. Your project is kept there as ordinary files.
  3. Go to Inputs and drop in an IFC model and a P6 programme (.xer or .xml).
  4. Optionally press Install in the address bar, so the app opens from your desktop and works offline.

Check a programme

About 10 minutes. The full text is in the documentation.

  1. Load the programme on Inputs, then open the Programmes tab.
  2. Switch between the table, Gantt and the other views to see the same activities in different ways.
  3. Use Filters, Group, Sort and Colour in the side pane to find what you are after. Click an activity to open its details on the right.
  4. Look at float and the critical path. They come from the file when it has them, and are calculated from the programme's calendars when it does not.
  5. Open Resources to see labour demand against capacity, and click a bar to see the activities behind a peak.

Plan the next few weeks

About 5 minutes. The full text is in the documentation.

  1. On the Programmes page, choose Lookahead from the Views list.
  2. Pick the first day (it starts on the data date) and the length, from 1 to 12 weeks.
  3. Read the list: every unfinished activity that starts, finishes or runs through the window, plus everything already late. A tag says why each is listed.
  4. Press Export (CSV) to save the list, or Set 4D date to move the 4D timeline to the first day.

Link a programme to a model and play it in 4D

About 15 minutes. The full text is in the documentation.

  1. Load the model and the programme on Inputs.
  2. Open 4D Sequencing and make a link group. Pick the tasks with task filters, and the objects with object filters.
  3. Add a match rule that pairs a task field with an object property, for example the task's Zone with the object's Level. Check the counts: a group that links nothing shows zero.
  4. Set an appearance profile: whether objects install, are removed or are maintained, and their colour and opacity before, during and after the task.
  5. Press play on the timeline, or step and scrub by day, week or month. Choose planned or actual dates.
  6. Use Export to save the sequence as PNG images or an MP4 video made in your browser.

Change many activities at once

About 5 minutes. The full text is in the documentation.

  1. Choose Bulk edit from the Views list on the Programmes page.
  2. Under Which activities, add rules on any column (contains, equals, starts with, is empty…).
  3. Under What to change, choose the edit: set a user field, find and replace in names, shift dates, change durations, set a constraint or set the calendar.
  4. Read the preview, then press Apply. Undo the last edit puts them back, and Download Excel keeps the result.
