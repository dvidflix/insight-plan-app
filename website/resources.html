System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11
  • Chrome or Edge
  • 16 GB of memory recommended for large models
  • Python 3.12 only if you use the experimental programme generator
  • No internet connection needed to run

Data handling

Programmes, models and results are read and saved in the project folder you choose, on your computer. The app does not send them anywhere and has no account or telemetry. A third-party software list ships with each release.

Frequently asked

Which programme formats can it read?

Primavera P6 XER and XML, and spreadsheet and CSV exports. It also writes XER and XML.

Which model formats?

IFC. Models are converted and drawn in the app on your own machine.

Can it be used for safety-related decisions?

No. It is a planning and review tool. It does not replace your safety case or approved engineering processes.

How do updates work?

You receive a new version as a folder or installer and install it when you choose. The app does not update itself over the internet.

Will there be a multi-user, cloud version?

The weekly planning module for site teams is in development and is designed for tablets and several users at once. The planning workbench stays offline.
