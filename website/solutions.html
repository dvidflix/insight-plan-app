Problems it solves

The recurring questions planners are asked, and where the answer usually gets lost between a programme, a model and a spreadsheet.

1

The programme and the model say different things

Activities and model objects are matched by hand, in spreadsheets, and the match breaks the moment the programme is revised.

How Insight Plan helps: Link groups connect activities to objects by rules on their properties. After a revision they re-run, and a check shows the activities and objects left unlinked.

2

Nobody sees the concrete and rebar peaks until the site does

A programme can look sensible while asking for twice the normal pour or rebar volume in a few weeks.

How Insight Plan helps: Weekly and cumulative concrete and rebar charts come straight from the programme's resources. Overlay a baseline and a new draft to see where they differ.

3

What did the latest revision actually change?

Comparing two programmes by eye, or by exporting both to Excel, takes days and still misses things.

How Insight Plan helps: Overlay two programmes and their curves, compare dates, and try what-if changes to see their effect before you commit to one.

4

The sequence is hard to explain to people who do not read Gantt charts

Project managers, clients and construction teams need to see the order of work, not a bar chart.

How Insight Plan helps: Play the build forward in 4D and export images or video. Show two options side by side in the same view, or use the compare slider.

5

The tools want your data on someone else's server

Sensitive projects cannot upload models and programmes to a cloud service, or wait for IT to approve one.

How Insight Plan helps: It runs on the planner's own laptop with no internet connection. No account, no telemetry, and your files stay in your project folder.

6

Sharing a programme without sharing the project

A programme often has to go to a third party with names, codes and people removed.

How Insight Plan helps: An anonymise tool replaces names and codes consistently so a programme can be shared safely.
