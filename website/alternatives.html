Where it fits next to the tools you know

Honest differences with 10 alternatives and with doing it by hand, and the cases where another tool is the better choice.

In short

One by one

Bentley SYNCHRO 4D

What it offers

Bentley lists 4D scheduling and simulation, what-if scenarios, baseline against actual progress, clash detection, and schedules built in the tool or brought in from P6 and Asta Powerproject. It is a subscription product sold through Bentley and its resellers.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan is analysis-first: it checks the programme and the model, re-runs links after a revision, and finds missing predecessors from the model's geometry, in a browser with no server. It needs no model-preparation project.

In practice: Choose SYNCHRO when 4D simulation is a managed discipline across a large team. Use both when you want the programme and model checked before they go in.

Autodesk Navisworks

What it offers

TimeLiner links a model to a schedule for 4D playback, and Clash Detective is in Manage but not in Simulate. It reads many model formats and is widely used by coordinators.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan works on the programme itself: DCMA checks, comparison, resource peaks, risk and look-ahead, which Navisworks does not cover. Links are rules that re-run after a revision rather than selection sets you keep by hand. It writes a TimeLiner pack, so it can feed Navisworks.

In practice: Keep Navisworks for federated model coordination. Use us to prepare and check the programme and links that go into TimeLiner.

Microsoft Project

What it offers

Microsoft sells Project as plans for building and managing schedules: Gantt charts, task dependencies and critical path, and resource management, with desktop and web apps and a link to the wider Microsoft 365 tools. Prices are per user and published by Microsoft. It has no model view of its own, so 4D tools read its files.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan does not ask you to move your scheduling into a new tool. It reads MS Project, P6 and Excel programmes and adds what a scheduler does not: DCMA checks, revision comparison, resource and concrete peaks, and the link to the model for 4D, clash detection and IDS checks.

In practice: Keep MS Project to build and update the programme. Use us to check it, compare revisions and test it against the model.

Fuzor

What it offers

Kalloc lists 4D sequence animation, clash analysis, VR viewing and method statements, P6, MS Project and Powerproject schedule import, and an AI-assisted workflow for linking models to schedules in the 2024 release.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan is not a visualisation studio. It is for the planner who must be sure the programme and model agree: quality checks, model-based predecessors, concrete and rebar peaks, and revision comparison, with nothing leaving the computer.

In practice: Choose Fuzor for immersive presentation and method statements. Use us for the checking behind the story.

cmBuilder

What it offers

cmBuilder lists drag-and-drop 2D and 3D site logistics planning, a library of more than 3,000 construction resources, excavation modelling, 4D simulation, import of programmes, BIM data, drone scans and drawings, quantities by phase, real-time multi-user editing with unlimited users, and live share links for stakeholders. It is a subscription product, billed annually, with published tiers from USD 416 a month.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan does not lay out the site or model cranes and hoarding. It checks the programme against the model: DCMA checks, missing predecessors found from the model's geometry, links by rule that re-run after a revision, resource peaks and revision comparison, with programmes and models kept on your computer.

In practice: Choose cmBuilder to plan and present site logistics and temporary works, especially in preconstruction. Use us to check that the programme behind the simulation agrees with the permanent works.

Oracle Primavera P6 and Risk Analysis

What it offers

P6 builds and controls the programme. Oracle sells Primavera Risk Analysis as a separate product for Monte Carlo risk, with P6 data exported to it.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan reads and writes P6 XER and XML, and adds the model link, 4D, clash detection and quality checks P6 does not do. Monte Carlo risk and what-if are included, and are still marked experimental.

In practice: Not a replacement. Keep P6 to build the programme and use us to investigate it.

Solibri

What it offers

Solibri checks models against rule sets and supports IDS from release 9.13.6. Which licence tier includes IDS depends on Solibri, so check the current plans.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan's IDS checks come with fixes: preview a patch, then write a corrected copy of the IFC and keep the original. They sit beside the programme, so a failed check can be traced to the activities it affects.

In practice: Choose Solibri for a deep, rule-driven model quality programme. Use us when the question is how model quality affects the programme.

Simplebim

What it offers

Datacubist describes Simplebim as software that imports and merges IFC models, adds missing data such as calculated quantities, generated objects and external data, classifies and groups elements, normalises property values, and exports to IFC, CSV and Excel through reusable dataflows. Its published pages list production planning and scheduling as use cases, not as functions. Licence prices are not published, and a free trial is offered.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan does not restructure or enrich model data. It reads the IFC as it is, checks it with IDS, fixes what fails in a copy, and then checks the programme against the model: DCMA checks, missing predecessors, links by rule, 4D and resource peaks.

In practice: Choose Simplebim to prepare, classify and enrich the model data that everyone else will use. Use us once the model and programme meet, to check that they agree.

Speckle

What it offers

Speckle describes an "intelligent design data layer for AEC": connectors for Revit, Rhino, Tekla, Civil 3D, Archicad, Navisworks, IFC and more, a viewer for version-controlled models, model data checks, dashboards, Speckle Automate and an AI layer that answers questions from published model data. It has a free plan (one project, up to three users) and custom paid plans. Its pricing and home pages do not list programmes, scheduling or 4D.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan starts from the programme, which Speckle's published pages do not cover: DCMA checks, revision comparison, resource and concrete peaks, links by rule and 4D playback. It reads IFC, runs IDS checks and clash detection, and keeps programmes and models on your computer instead of a cloud service.

In practice: Choose Speckle to move data between authoring tools and share live models across a design team. Use us when the question is how the programme and the model agree.

ALICE Technologies

What it offers

ALICE Technologies lists generative scheduling, targeted optimisation (where a schedule can be accelerated, and by how much), construction sequencing and simulation, optimisation presets, a schedule quality score, analytics and reporting, and an assistant you chat with about the schedule. It is a cloud service sold by quotation.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan does not generate or optimise a programme with AI. It checks the programme you have against the model: DCMA checks, missing predecessors found from the model's geometry, links by rule that re-run after a revision, 4D playback, clash and IDS checks, with programmes and models kept on your computer.

In practice: Choose ALICE to let an AI search for a faster or cheaper way to build a project. Use us when the question is whether the programme and the model agree.

Spreadsheets and doing it by hand

What it offers

Free, flexible and familiar. Matching activities to objects, comparing revisions and building the story for a meeting is manual, slow and easy to get wrong.

How Insight Plan differs

Insight Plan keeps your files and formats, and replaces the repetitive part: links by rule, revisions compared in minutes, checks with evidence.

In practice: The easiest comparison to make. The pricing page shows the break-even time.

When another tool is the better choice

We would rather you picked the right tool.

You build and control the programme

Primavera P6 or MS Project. Insight Plan reads and writes the files, it does not replace them.

You need photoreal visuals, VR or crane animation for a bid

Fuzor, SYNCHRO or a rendering tool. Insight Plan plays the sequence, with images and video, but it is built for checking, not for show.

You plan site logistics, cranes and temporary works

cmBuilder, SYNCHRO or Fuzor. Insight Plan does not lay out the site or model temporary works.

You run federated coordination across many disciplines and formats

Navisworks or a common data environment. Insight Plan reads IFC.

You need many people editing the same live project in the cloud

Trimble Connect, Speckle or a similar cloud platform. Insight Plan keeps your data on your machine and is single-user by design.
