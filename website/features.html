Features

Everything between the programme and the model

One tool to load, link, analyse and present. Nothing to install on a server, nothing sent to the cloud.

Link

Activities and objects, joined by rules.

Link groups connect activities to model objects by rules on their properties, so a programme revision re-links without starting over, and a check lists what is left unlinked.

  • Works on the properties your model already has
  • Re-runs after every revision
Sequence

4D, with the programme beside it.

Play the build forward date by date, with the Gantt chart and the weekly numbers in the same window. Compare two sequences side by side or with a slider.

  • Images and video with date labels and captions
  • Save a scene: camera, split view and filters

Analyse

Concrete and rebar by week.

Quantities come straight from the resources in the programme. Overlay a baseline and a new draft, group by week, month, quarter or year, and colour the lines as you like.

  • Weekly bars and cumulative lines
  • Export the chart as an image, the data as CSV

And the rest of the toolbox

Programme analysis, model tools and exports, working on the same files.

Programme analysis

Gantt, WBS, calendars, critical path, resource loading and checks, on XER and XML programmes. Group, filter, sort and colour with the same rules throughout.

Programme comparison

Overlay programmes and their curves to see what a revision changed, and try what-if changes to see their effect.

Colour, filter and isolate

Colour the model by any property, hide or isolate parts, and order the legend the way you want it.

Reports and data exports

Excel, CSV, P6 XER and XML, and a Navisworks TimeLiner pack, so your data goes out in formats other tools read.

Images and video

Export 4D sequences as images or video, from one or two views.

Programme generator Experimental

Generate a pour-by-pour programme from the model, your crews and rules, then adjust it by hand and compare the options. Switched on in Settings.

Run a pilot on a real project

Bring a live programme and its model. We will set up a short, fixed-price pilot and show what it finds.
