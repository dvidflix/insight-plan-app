Features

Everything between the programme and the model

One tool to load, link, analyse and present. Nothing to install on a server, nothing sent to the cloud.

The difference

Check the programme against the model, not just against itself.

Insight Plan reads where objects sit in the model: which pour is below, which wall stands beside. Where the model says one thing depends on another and the programme has no link, it lists the missing predecessor with the contact it found.

  • Missing predecessors for each activity, with the evidence: below or beside, pairs touching, closest gap
  • Built for concrete pours and walls; the same contacts also feed clash detection
  • Play the corrected sequence in 4D so everyone sees the consequence

Link

Activities and objects, joined by rules.

Link groups connect activities to model objects by rules on their properties, so a programme revision re-links without starting over, and a check lists what is left unlinked.

  • Works on the properties your model already has
  • Re-runs after every revision

Analyse

Concrete and rebar by week.

Quantities come straight from the resources in the programme. Overlay a baseline and a new draft, group by week, month, quarter or year, and colour the lines as you like.

  • Weekly bars and cumulative lines
  • Export the chart as an image, the data as CSV
Clash detection

Find what touches, within a model or across models.

Every model is checked against itself and against every other loaded model, on the real geometry and where the models are placed. Each result names both objects, says whether they overlap or only touch, and shows the gap. Click a pair to see it in 3D.

  • Within one model, or between two models
  • Set the tolerance, test real triangles or boxes only
  • Results are saved in the project and refresh only when a model or its placement changes
  • The same pairs feed the programme generator, which uses them to order pours

Works with your scheduler, or without one

Use Insight Plan alongside the tools you have, or on its own.

Round trip with Primavera P6

Reads P6 XER and XML, and a P6 Professional database live and read-only. Writes P6 XER and XML back, so your changes return to P6 by import. Nothing is ever written into P6 directly.

Reads MS Project

Open a programme saved from Microsoft Project as XML, with its tasks and links, and use it for 4D, resources and checks.

Excel and CSV

No scheduler at all? Drop a spreadsheet with names and dates and it becomes a programme you can link to the model.

Or no programme at all

Models alone work too: clashes, IDS checks and patches, search sets, the set player, colouring and measuring.

MS Project files are read, not written. Save from MS Project as XML. P6 and Microsoft Project are trademarks of their owners.

Beyond Primavera P6

Insight Plan reads and writes P6 programmes, and adds what P6 does not do on its own.

3D and 4D in one place

P6 holds the programme only. Here the programme is linked to the IFC model and played forward date by date.

Clash detection

Overlaps and contacts within a model and between models, kept up to date as models change.

Rules-based linking

Link activities to model objects by property rules that re-run after each revision, with a check for what is left unlinked.

Model checks (IDS) and BCF topics

Check a model against IDS requirements and fix failures. Raise topics (BCF issues) with status, priority, an assignee and comments with viewpoints, place them in the model, and exchange them as BCF files.

Concrete and rebar peaks

Weekly and cumulative quantities from the programme resources, overlaid on a baseline.

Look-ahead and PPC

Look-ahead windows and Percent Plan Complete with reasons for misses, from the activities already in the programme.

Risk and what-if on your own computer

Monte Carlo finish dates and what-if changes, with no extra risk product.

Anonymise and share

Mask names and codes consistently so a programme can leave the company safely.

DCMA quality checks

The DCMA 14-point check with editable thresholds, plus extra checks and reports.

Comparison is with P6 as a stand-alone scheduler. Other Oracle products and add-ons may cover some of these. Primavera and P6 are trademarks of Oracle.
