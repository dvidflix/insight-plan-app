About us

Built from years of experience on site

Insight Plan is made for the planners who have to explain, check and defend a construction programme.

Why it exists

Programmes and models describe the same building, and they drift apart. Matching them by hand costs days, and the mismatch usually shows up on site. The tools that could help often want your data on someone else's server. We built the tool we wanted: one that reads the files you already have, checks them against each other, and keeps everything on your machine.

Who it is for

Planners and project controls teams on complex construction, and the engineers and managers who rely on what they say. Particularly where data handling matters, such as nuclear and other regulated heavy civil work.

What we hold to

Your data stays yours

No account, no telemetry, and no upload. Programmes and models stay in your project folder, in open formats.

Show the evidence

Every finding comes with the objects, the gap or the threshold behind it, so you can check it and stand behind it.

Honest about limits

It is a planning and review tool, not a safety system. Features still being refined are marked experimental, and we say when another tool is the better choice.

Simple to run

No server and no IT project. Install it from the browser, and it works offline.

How it is built

Open formats, no server, and nothing you cannot check.

Open formats

Programmes stay as XER, XML or Excel, models as IFC, and the project as a SQLite file in your own folder. If you stop, you keep everything.

Runs where you are

A web app that installs from Chrome or Edge and works offline. A licence key is checked on your computer, so there is no sign-in and no licence server.

Working you can check

Checks and links come with the objects, activities and thresholds behind them, so a result can be traced and defended.

What is next

  • Weekly planning for the site team: a tablet-friendly look-ahead with percentage planned complete, working from the activities already in your programme.
  • Viewer-only access for colleagues who only need to look at results.
